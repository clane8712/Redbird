Redbird drop alert emails · rough draft

Real drop data from the week of Oct 19. Links and the sending address are placeholders while we test. Each email works on phones and desktop.

1 · Confirm sign-up
Subject: Confirm your Redbird drop alerts

Sent right after someone signs up. Nothing else goes out until they tap the button, which also keeps bots off the list.

2 · Shop alert
Subject: Fresh Redbird at 3 of your shops

For people following shops. One email covers every shop they follow that got a drop, at most one alert email a day.

3 · Strain alert
Subject: 3 strains you follow just landed

For people following strains at any shop. One email covers every strain they follow that landed, with the shops closest to their zip.
